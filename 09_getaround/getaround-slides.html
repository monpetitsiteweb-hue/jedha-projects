The problem
Brief · Two decisions · Three deliverables

Late returns make the next driver wait. A delay fixes it, but costs rentals.

“Getaround decided to implement a minimum delay between two rentals. [...] It solves the late checkout issue but also potentially hurts Getaround/owners revenues.”Project brief, Getaround

Two decisions for the Product Manager

  • Threshold: how long, in minutes?
  • Scope: all cars, or only Connect cars (opened with a phone, no owner present)?

And one more team

  • Suggest a daily price for a car, from its features

The deliverables

  • A dashboard in production
  • An online API, with POST /predict and documentation at /docs
  • The whole code on GitHub
Two notebooks, in the order of the brief: 01 delays and simulation → the dashboard · 02 price model → the API. Each app has its own folder and Dockerfile.

Limits & choices

What the numbers do not say

Known limits

  • Revenue is estimated: no price in the rentals file, and drivers who rebook another slot are not measured
  • The model prices comparable cars: without demand data, it does not maximise revenue
  • The deployed model is retrained on all cars, so it has no clean test score of its own
  • Cancellations: 17 % vs 11 % is a link, the file gives no reason

Choices I defend

  • 120 min, not 60: 60 min solves only 47 % of the cases, 120 min 67 %
  • All cars, not Connect only: Connect can solve at most 69 of 218 cases
  • Gradient boosting, tied with random forest, kept for speed and a smaller model
  • Model saved with joblib inside the Docker image; MLflow's model registry is the next step

Takeaways
Conclusion

My recommendation: all cars, 120 minutes. Most of the problem solved, for a limited cost

  • All cars, 120 minutes: 67 % of the problematic cases solved, at most 3.3 % of revenue exposed. Beyond that, each extra minute brings less
  • The Product Manager has an online tool to test any other setting
  • The price API answers in under a second, documented, reproducible: same scores when re-run three weeks after training

Next steps

  • Add the price of each rental to the delays file, to count the revenue lost in euros instead of a share of rentals
  • Try the 120-minute delay on half of the cars for a few weeks, and compare waiting, cancellations and real revenue with the other half
  • Register the model in MLflow, so that each version is kept and deployed from there
